Music

Henry Jackman composed the score for the film.[71] The soundtrack features an original song titled "Immortals" written and recorded by American rock band Fall Out Boy, which was released by Walt Disney Records on October 14, 2014.[72][73] The soundtrack album was digitally released by Walt Disney Records on November 4, 2014, and had a CD release on November 25.[74] While not part of the soundtrack, a brief instrumental section of "Eye of the Tiger" plays in the film.[75] For the Japanese release of the album, a previously unreleased English version of Japanese-American singer Ai's single "Story" was released as a single by EMI Records and Walt Disney Records exclusively in Japan.[76][77] The song was used in promotional videos and the film's credits for the Japanese release.[78]

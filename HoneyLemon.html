HONEY LEMON

Background

Not much is known about Honey Lemon, although in the Season 2 episode "Something's Fishy" of Big Hero 6: The Series, Honey Lemon says high school is where she fell in love with chemistry, also mentioning how she fell in love with French exchange student Andre. As a child, she had taste for clothing and cooking. She also experimented in her family's tool shed (which suffered terrible consequences) with chemicals found under the kitchen sink.[2]

She also participated in "The Nation's Got Flavor", and ended up as a 2nd season runner-up.[2]

Her passion for chemistry soon led her to enrollment at San Fransokyo Institute of Technology with the goal of introducing energy-efficient, environmentally friendly chemical formulas.

Personality

Honey is a tremendously intelligent, self-proclaimed "chemistry whiz" and an incredibly bubbly and optimistic woman as cute as her namesake rolled into an exuberant ball of energy. Arguably the most extroverted member of Big Hero 6, Honey is always upbeat, perky, optimistic, cute, caring and outgoing; when she's around, she brings everyone into a better mood and alleviates conflict. Honey loves living the experience with her friends and seems to find everything in life interesting, thus she never finds a melancholic or dull moment. She is humorously photogenic and tends to take pictures and selfies with her friends whenever she finds a moment that excites her. Despite her cute personality, Honey can occasionally lose her temper.

Alongside her bubbly nature, Honey's intelligence is profound and possesses an intimate passion for chemistry and nature, to the point of eccentricity. Coupling this with her enthusiasm, she has an unexpected love of danger and experiencing surprising and sudden events. This is demonstrated by the fact that handling hazardous and volatile substances in her line of work does not faze her at all - on the contrary, she is excited to take part in it.

Of all her teammates, Honey is also the most observant. Throughout their time as heroes, she managed to interpret many other pieces of evidence regarding the identity and motives of Yokai, having found his refurbished portal, as well as a recording to show his true intentions. This arguably relates to the fact that Honey is the most intelligent member of the team, aside from Hiro.

According to supplemental materials, Honey is very environmental, as her passion for chemistry also comes from her desire to benefit the world. She believes in using chemistry to one day replace industrial and potentially pollutive methods with simple and safer applications.

Though she is known to be a lover of chemistry, she also developed a love for art, but kept this a secret from her friends because science and art were considered opposite sides by the students of San Fransokyo Institute of Technology and the San Fransokyo Art Institute; as well as the fact that both schools are rival institutions. She unknowingly revealed this to Go Go since she sleep-talks and eventually, all of her friends found this out, but were accepting and had without a issue.

Appearance

Honey Lemon is a young Latina woman of rather tall and slim stature, even without her trademark platform shoes. She has light tan skin, green eyes and long, straight honey-blonde hair (hence her name) that reaches halfway down her back. For some reason, the end credits and in the TV series depicts her with more ginger-colored hair, perhaps to make her yellow color scheme stand out more.

Her hair is often held back by some kind of headband, and she wears a pair of large magenta-framed glasses which also functions as her impromptu safety goggles. She also wears light makeup that simply consists of pink lipstick.

In terms of attire, Honey Lemon often changes clothing throughout the movie, showcasing the audience that her wardrobe consists of clothing that are 1960s and early 1970s-styled, many which are often in different bright colors such as yellows, pinks and oranges. At the very least, her usual one is a yellow headband, a long yellow cardigan-coat over a short, sleeveless, brighter-yellow dress with a white peter-pan collar and a set of white sheer tights, orange calf-length socks with frilled-cuffs, and yellow Mary Jane platform heels.

While encountering Yokai for the first time, she wore a large white peter-pan collared sweater with a pink ribbon and a headband in the same color. She wears a red, pink and beige horizontal-striped skirt with pink polka-dotted sheer leggings.

At Tadashi's funeral, she wears a black long-sleeved dress with a belt and a white peter-pan collar with black tights and her hair up in a modest bun.

In her superhero form, Honey Lemon's costume consists of a latex, high-collared dark magenta under-suit with finger holes which is under a red-and-fuchsia armor-like dress with thin white linings, a red belt around the waist, and magenta boots with pink laces and wedge heels. She never dons her glasses, instead wearing a helmet that is the same color as her dress but with orange accents. Hung over her shoulder is her special purse for combat, in which there is a keyboard depicting the periodic table so that she can come up with a formula to create her chem-balls. Out of the team, her suit is the least armored, but this keeps her nimble and agile when attacking.

In the film, her pink helmet has a transparent visor that fully covers her face, although in Big Hero 6: The Series, it's retconned as a pink-tinted visor that only covers her eyes instead.

Power and Abilities

Like her teammates, Honey Lemon does not have superpowers, but possesses high intelligence that more than makes up for it. In her case, she is an exceptional chemist with encyclopedic knowledge of the periodic table of elements. She also has above-average agility and strength, though not as high as her teammate Go Go Tomago.

  • Multilingualism: She is fluent in English, Spanish and Japanese.[2]
  • Expert chemistry skills: Honey is a brilliant chemist, able to calculate a plethora of chemical substances with a variety of effect. At the university, she was even able to induce chemical embrittlement to a four-hundred pound ball of tungsten carbide, an incredibly dense substance, turning it to dust by mere touch afterwards. Her knowledge in chemistry is thorough enough for her to calculate and input custom chemicals on her purse at incredible speeds without having to looking at the typepad.

    • Equipment

    Honey is equipped with special features and weapons from her suit, making her the most versatile member of the team. The suit also protects her skin from potential chemical leaks.

  • Chem-Purse: The chem-purse is Honey's entire arsenal, as it allows her to create spherical grenade-like capsules called chem-balls.
  • Machine gun mode: Hiro created a modified chem-purse with a machine gun mode that allows her to shoot chem-balls at a faster pace like a paintball cannon.
  • Sniper-bazooka mode: In "City of Monsters", the modified chem-purse has another mode in the opposite side: this one is used as a bazooka using the canon of the machine gun support, deploying a sight in the side, allowing Honey make a long-distance accurate shot.
  • Chem-Balls: Like their name implies, chem-balls are hand-held paintball pellets roughly the size of lacrosse balls filled and prepped with all kinds of chemical compounds. By mixing and crossing any elemental compound her chem-purse contains, these chem-balls are capable of serving any conceivable function within certain parameters, enabling her to solve any problem she's confronted with, which primarily includes head-on projectile weaponry or forming attack constructs.
  • Bunny Shield: She can encase herself in a gelatinous substance shaped like a bunny that helps to shield her from attacks.
  • Chem-boots: Equipped on Honey's ultra armor, these boots can shoot chem-balls at a close distance through a hole in the soles, permitting the use of different chemicals to upgraded mobility:
  • Goop: creates a gelatinous chemical for Honey to bounce off and make large jumps.
  • Freezing: Creates ice behind the boots, allowing Honey to slide towards any place, inclusive in midair.

Concept

Originally in Marvel Comics, Aiko Miyazaki was a Japanese scientist working for the Naichō. Her research team discovered a way to create miniature inter-universe wormholes, and at her suggestion, created the "Power Purse" to contain these wormholes. Upon being told that the Giri Corporation was recruiting individuals to form a superhero team, Aiko convinced her superiors to grant her a spot in it. She adopted the code name Honey Lemon in reference to the title of her favorite TV show, and utilized the Power Purse as her primary weapon.

For the movie, Honey Lemon was first conceived as a colorful, outgoing fashionista, but to emphasize the idea that she really comes into her own as a superhero, her regular clothes were toned down so her bright orange supersuit was a big contrast. Director Don Hall mentioned that they wanted her to be very smart and ground her Power Purse in science. Her personality was a "tough one to crack", as they wanted her to have a contrast in being a character who is sweet and innocent on the outside, but inside has a darkness, and likes to destroy things[4].

Early information regarding the movie's plot had called for Honey and Go Go having a shallow rivalry involving boys. Honey was also depicted as a pyromaniac (or in general, a secret love for blowing things up), who was very concerned about her clothes[5].

Other pieces of concept art showed Honey Lemon as a barista at the Lucky Cat Café (or Sweet Bean Coffee in some early drafts of the film). One of these early concept designs made it into the film in the form of a San Fransokyo advertisement billboard that can be seen very briefly at the beginning of the film, and when Baymax and Hiro test their flying abilities in the city for the first time.
